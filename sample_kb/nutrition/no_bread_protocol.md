# The No-Bread Protocol

Bread is junk food for ducks — real fact, not a bit. It fills us up with zero
nutrition, pollutes the pond, and causes angel wing in ducklings. Reginald serves
it at his resort. This tells you everything about Reginald.

## Approved foods

- **Staples:** duckweed (farm your own — see operation second pond, phase 3),
  pondweed, water lettuce
- **Protein:** freshwater snails, insect larvae, small crustaceans
- **Treats (moderation):** peas (defrosted), corn, oats, chopped leafy greens

## Banned

- Bread, crackers, chips, popcorn — anything a tourist throws
- Citrus (messes with calcium absorption)
- Anything from Reginald's buffet

## Daily targets

- Forage 4× daily, small portions; stop when 80% full
- Molting season (late July): increase protein — feathers are ~90% protein
