# Operation Second Pond

The plan: build a better pond than Reginald's and let the flock vote with their wings.

## Background

Reginald inherited the family pond through a technicality involving grandfather's will
and a suspiciously well-timed heron. The flock scattered. I do not want revenge in the
petty sense. I want a nicer pond, fuller of my friends, directly upstream of his.

## Phases

| Phase | Milestone | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scout candidate ponds (min. 40m diameter, low heron traffic) | ✅ done — shortlist of 3 |
| 2 | Secure the east creek site | 🔄 negotiating with the beavers |
| 3 | Duckweed cultivation program | ⏳ blocked on phase 2 |
| 4 | Flock recruitment drive | 🔄 7/50 recruited |
| 5 | Grand opening before winter migration | ⏳ |

## Recruitment pipeline

- 7 confirmed (cousin Mildred + her brood).
- 12 warm leads from the autumn flyover meetup.
- Reginald's resort reviews are dropping (2.5 stars — "too much bread, no depth").
  Every 1-star review is a recruitment opportunity.

## Risks

- The beavers want 15% of the duckweed yield. Counter-offered 8%.
- Reginald may undercut with free bread. This is actually good for us long-term
  (see nutrition/no_bread_protocol.md — bread is a trap).
