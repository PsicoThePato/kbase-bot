# Flight Training — Migration Prep

Goal: migration-grade conditioning by October. Left wing still in phased return
after the regatta incident — no max-effort flapping until cleared.

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Monday | Endurance: 3 pond laps at conversational quack pace |
| Tuesday | V-formation drills with Mildred's brood (7am, east bank) |
| Wednesday | Rest + preening |
| Thursday | Intervals: 6 × 30s sprint glides, full recovery between |
| Friday | Technique: water landings, crosswind approaches |

| Sunday | Rest. Non-negotiable. |

## Left wing protocol

- Warm up with 2 minutes of gentle stretching — never skip.
- Pain above 3/10 → stop, ice (sit in the cold part of the pond), log it.
- No aerobatics until the vet duck signs off.

## Morning mobility (daily)

Neck rolls, wing circles, tail wags — 5 minutes before first swim.
